- **study_focus:** Paranoid social inference; social learning under uncertainty; trait inference about others' harmful intent
- **study_focus_short:** Paranoid social inference · social learning under uncertainty
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from a partner's economic decisions about the partner's harmful intent and self-interest over repeated social exchanges.   - Learning from:     - Source type (social): other (dictator partner)     - Source content (social): outcomes (monetary split decisions)   - Learning about:     - Target type (social): other (dictator partner)     - Target content (social): state (mental state; harmful intent and self-interest attributions)
- **task_description:** Participants completed the Green Paranoid Thoughts Scale (GPTS) then played a modified serial Dictator Game with three partners (fair, partially fair, unfair) for 6 rounds each (18 total), rating harmful intent and self-interest attributions (0-100) after each exchange.
- **task_paradigm:** Dictator game
- **players:** Single agent (participant), multi-target (3 dictator partners: fair, partially fair, unfair; real human decisions via ex-post matching)
- **n_players:** multi-target (3+)
- **partner_type:** computer (algorithmic)
- **stimuli:** Cartoon avatars with neutral expressions; monetary splits (take half or all of 10p); 0-100 VAS scales for harmful intent and self-interest attributions
- **method:** behavioural (online)
- **main_result:** - Pre-existing paranoia associated with greater uncertainty about partner's policy (uPI: beta = 0.09, 95% CI: 0.08, 0.10) - Pre-existing paranoia associated with greater baseline uncertainty over harmful intent (uHI0: beta = 0.04, 95% CI: 0.02, 0.05) and self-interest (uSI0: beta = 0.03, 95% CI: 0.01, 0.04) - Higher harmful intent attributions associated with greater uHI0 (beta = 0.35, 95% CI: 0.31, 0.39), lower learning rates (beta = -0.20, 95% CI: -0.34, -0.07), and greater policy uncertainty (beta = 0.12, 95% CI: 0.08, 0.16) - Higher self-interest attributions associated with lower uSI0 (beta = -0.29, 95% CI: -0.31, -0.27) and lower policy uncertainty (beta = -0.13, 95% CI: -0.16, -0.10) - Network modelling: paranoia moderated pHI0-eta (more negative) and pHI0-uPI (more positive) edges - Simulated data highly correlated with real data (HI: rho = 0.83-0.90; SI: rho = 0.64-0.70)
- **effect_size:** - uPI ~ paranoia: beta = 0.09 (95% CI: 0.08, 0.10) - uHI0 ~ paranoia: beta = 0.04 (95% CI: 0.02, 0.05) - uSI0 ~ paranoia: beta = 0.03 (95% CI: 0.01, 0.04) - Simulated HI ~ paranoia (unfair dictator): beta = 0.22 (95% CI: 0.18, 0.25) - Simulated HI ~ uHI0: beta = 0.35 (95% CI: 0.31, 0.39) - Simulated HI ~ eta: beta = -0.20 (95% CI: -0.34, -0.07) - Mean log-likelihood: -2.66 (median: -2.45)

- **model_params:** - pHI0: baseline probability of harmful intent attribution (mode of prior); no group mean reported - pSI0: baseline probability of self-interest attribution (mode of prior); no group mean reported - uHI0: baseline uncertainty of harmful intent (inverse precision of prior); no group mean reported - uSI0: baseline uncertainty of self-interest (inverse precision of prior); no group mean reported - uPI [S]: uncertainty over partner's policy (how consistently partner attributes map to behaviors); no group mean reported - eta (eta): learning rate from one dictator to the next (admixture of posterior into prior for next partner); no group mean reported
