- **ecological_validity:** Low — artificial social networks with stock photos and shuffled club labels; cover story framed as real undergraduate friendships; monetary incentive to remember friendships but not features; lab-based study
- **eligibility_flag:** Study 1 does not include computational modeling (only representational similarity analysis with linear regression). The computational modeling occurs in Studies 2 and 3. Flag: "Study 1 lacks formal computational model; RSA only.
