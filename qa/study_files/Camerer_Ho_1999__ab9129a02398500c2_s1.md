## Algorithmic level
- **winning_model:** EWA (Experience-Weighted Attraction): A^j_i(t) = [phi * N(t-1) * A^j_i(t-1) + [delta + (1-delta) * I(s^j_i, s_i(t))] * pi(s^j_i, s_{-i}(t))] / N(t), with N(t) = kappa * N(t-1) + 1. Choice via logit: P^j_i(t+1) = exp(lambda * A^j_i(t)) / sum_k exp(lambda * A^k_i(t)). Parameters: delta (foregone payoff weight), phi (attraction decay), kappa (experience decay), N(0) (initial experience weight), A^j(0) (initial attractions), lambda (payoff sensitivity).

## Quality
- **sample_size:** Constant-sum games: N=20 per game (10 pairs), 4 games; Median-action games: N=54 (six 9-person groups, 10 periods); p-beauty contests: N=196 undergraduates (seven-person groups, 10 periods twice). Total across all datasets: ~290 subjects.
- **population_category:** undergraduates
- **population_age_range:** 
- **ecological_validity:** Low. All tasks are abstract laboratory economic games with monetary payoffs. No naturalistic social interaction. Games use artificial payoff matrices and stylized strategic settings (constant-sum, coordination, beauty contest). However, the median-action game captures some coordination dynamics relevant to social conformity.
- **eligibility_flag:** MEDIUM confidence -- borderline social learning. The games involve human strategic interaction, but the models do not explicitly model social cognition, mentalizing, or social learning about other agents. The learning target is strategy optimality, not social information.
- **concerns:** - The "social" aspect is limited to strategic interaction; no explicit modeling of social cognition - Parameter values vary widely across game types, suggesting limited generalizability - Beauty contest model fits poorly (pseudo-R-squared ~7%), and EWA captures trends via inflation of initial attractions rather than genuine learning dynamics - No parameter recovery or model recovery analyses - Datasets come from different labs and countries (India, Singapore, USA) with different currencies and stakes - The paper is from 1999 (predates many current standards for computational modeling)
- **limitations_reported:** Authors acknowledge: EWA does not incorporate sophistication (players anticipating that others are learning); EWA requires imperfect payoff information to be handled by setting delta=0 rather than modeling it explicitly; stage-game strategies may not be the most natural strategy space (players may learn about history-dependent strategies or extrapolate trends); parameter values vary across games and a theory of how parameters depend on game structure is needed; beauty contest results show likely misspecification because adaptive models with stage-game strategies cannot capture trend extrapolation or sophisticated beliefs.
- **limitations_categorized:** Limited ecological validity; no parameter recovery; no model recovery; limited generalizability (parameters vary across games); model misspecification in some game types; no sophistication modeling; task simplicity (abstract laboratory games)
