## Computational level
- **study_focus:** How monetary, reputational, and responsibility consequences of advising modulate the relationship between experiential knowledge (via RL) and advice-giving behavior.
- **study_focus_short:** How monetary, reputational, and responsibility consequences of advising
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from own reward outcomes about option values, then deciding whether to share choice as advice under different consequence conditions   - Learning from:     - Source type (non-social): self     - Source content (non-social): outcome (fish yield / reward)   - Learning about:     - Target type (non-social): world (option/lake values)     - Target content (non-social): stimulus (reward value of options)
- **task_description:** Same 2-armed bandit fishing task as Study 1, but with four between-subjects conditions for advice consequences: No cost (replication), Monetary (small cost to advise), Reputation (public adviser rating), and Responsibility (advice compels future participants' choices).
- **task_paradigm:** Two-armed bandit
- **players:** Single agent (participant), unidirectional advice to future participants; 4 between-subjects conditions
- **n_players:** single agent (1)
- **partner_type:** none
- **stimuli:** Abstract lakes (2-armed bandit), numerical fish yield feedback, advice consequence framing (monetary cost / reputation rating / responsibility)
- **method:** online / behavioural
- **method_full:** Behavioural / online
- **main_result:** - Advice consequences modulated advising (AC main effect: χ²(3) = 56, p < .001) - Monetary cost strongly inhibited advice (mean advice: Money = 0.27, No cost = 0.60, Reputation = 0.76, Responsibility = 0.64) - P(choice) still predicted advice (OR = 8.17, χ²(1) = 666, p < .001) - P(choice) × AC interaction: χ²(3) = 80, p < .001 — monetary and reputational consequences flattened the relationship - P(choice) × SPIN interaction replicated (χ²(1) = 27, p < .001) - Triple interaction P(choice) × AC × SPIN: χ²(3) = 22, p < .001 - Triple interaction P(choice) × AC × PTM: χ²(3) = 25, p < .001 - Prosociality × P(choice) interaction emerged (OR = 0.40, χ²(1) = 4, p = .036)
