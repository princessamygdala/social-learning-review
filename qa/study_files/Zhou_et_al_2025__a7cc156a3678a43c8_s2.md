## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Ingroup bias learning; how framing interactions as gains vs. losses affects learning-related changes in intergroup impressions, moderated by ingroup identification, in a collectivistic culture.
- **study_focus_short:** Ingroup bias learning
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from monetary outcomes produced by ingroup/outgroup members about closeness to and impressions of those group members   - Learning from:     - Source type (social): other (ingroup or outgroup member)       - Source content (non-social): outcome (monetary gain/loss)   - Learning about:     - Target type (social): other (ingroup member) / other (outgroup member)       - Target content (social): state (mental state; impression/closeness)
- **task_description:** Chinese participants viewed pre-recorded choices from ingroup (Chinese) or outgroup (German) individuals who could give (Gain frame) or take (Loss frame) money, with identical net payoffs. Over 96 trials, participants rated closeness and expectancy, with impressions measured pre- and post-learning.
- **task_paradigm:** Impression formation task
- **players:** Single agent (participant), multi-target (6 ingroup Chinese individuals, 6 outgroup German individuals); between-subjects framing (Gain vs. Loss).
- **n_players:** multi-target (3+)
- **partner_type:** human (recorded)
- **stimuli:** Room labels (A/B) indicating ingroup/outgroup, pre-recorded monetary decisions (0 or 5 MU in Gain frame; 5 or 10 MU taken in Loss frame), manikin closeness scale.
- **method:** behavioural
- **method_full:** behavioural
- **main_result:** - No significant Frame x Group x Time interaction in East Asian sample (chi-squared = 1.01, p = 0.31, beta = 0.06) - Significant Group x Time interaction across both frames (chi-squared = 50.15, p < 0.001, beta = -0.35): learning reduced ingroup bias overall - Outgroup impressions improved (chi-squared = 29.62, p < 0.001, beta = 0.38); ingroup impressions decreased (chi-squared = 4.10, p = 0.043, beta = -0.16) - Frame x Ingroup identification interaction on impression change (beta = 0.41, t(95) = 2.96, p = 0.004): only low-identification participants showed frame-dependent bias reduction - Ingroup identification moderated W_neg_in -> impression change relationship (beta = 0.50, t(45) = 2.97, p = 0.005) - Significant Group x Valence interaction on learning rates (chi-squared = 5.20, p = 0.023): alpha_neg lower for ingroup than outgroup - Ingroup identification mediated culture effects on learning rate differences for negative PEs (indirect effect = 0.12, 95% CI [0.023, 0.225]) and positive PEs (indirect effect = -0.10, 95% CI [-0.18, -0.01])
- **effect_size:** - Group x Time: beta = -0.35 - Outgroup impression improvement: beta = 0.38 - Ingroup impression decrease: beta = -0.16 - Frame x Ingroup identification interaction: beta = 0.41 - Ingroup identification moderation of W_neg_in: beta = 0.50 - Cultural difference in ingroup identification: Cohen's d = 1.26
