## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Social reward learning — comparing neural substrates of reward learning for social (emotional faces) vs. monetary rewards during probabilistic instrumental learning.
- **study_focus_short:** Social reward learning
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from probabilistic reward outcomes (social or monetary) about the value of choice stimuli (slot machines)   - Learning from:     - Source type (non-social for monetary / social for social condition): world (slot machine outcomes)       - Monetary condition: non-social outcome (money gained/lost)       - Social condition: social outcome (happy/angry/neutral faces + affective words)     - Source content (non-social for monetary / social for social condition): outcome   - Learning about:     - Target type (non-social): world (slot machine stimulus values)     - Target content (non-social): stimulus (stimulus–reward associations)
- **task_description:** Participants played two structurally identical probabilistic instrumental learning tasks (one with monetary rewards, one with social rewards — happy/neutral/angry faces with matched affective words). On each trial, they chose between two slot machines associated with different outcome distributions and learned stimulus values by trial and error.
- **task_paradigm:** Probabilistic reward learning
- **players:** Single agent (participant), no social partner. Stimuli include faces but no interactive social agents.
- **n_players:** single agent (1)
- **partner_type:** human (recorded)
- **stimuli:** Cartoon slot machines varying in color/pattern; social outcomes: NimStim face photographs (happy/neutral/angry) with emotionally matched spoken words; monetary outcomes: images of dollar bill gained/lost/neutral.
- **method:** fMRI
- **method_full:** fMRI
- **main_result:** - Common vmPFC activation correlated with stimulus value (SV) at time of choice for both social and monetary conditions (conjunction: mOFC peak at 6, 27, −15; survives SVC P < 0.05) - Common vmPFC activation correlated with reward magnitude (R) at outcome for both conditions (conjunction: mOFC peak at −6, 36, −15; survives SVC P < 0.05) - Common ventral striatum activation correlated with prediction errors (PE) for monetary condition (putamen: −15, 6, −12, survives SVC P < 0.05); social PE did not survive omnibus threshold but showed striatal overlap at lower threshold (P < 0.005) - Behavioral: participants learned slower for social vs. monetary rewards and for negative vs. positive stimuli - Forced-choice control: vmPFC social and monetary reward signals of similar magnitude (P = 0.91), ruling out error-feedback confound
- **effect_size:** No formal effect sizes (Cohen's d, r, etc.) reported. Z-scores for key peaks: SV monetary vmPFC Z = 4.53; SV social vmPFC Z = 3.16; SV conjunction vmPFC Z = 3.16; PE monetary putamen Z = 4.07; R monetary vmPFC multiple clusters; R social vmPFC Z = 4.16; R conjunction vmPFC Z = 4.16.

## Quality
- **sample_size:** N = 22 (27 enrolled, 5 excluded: 4 for excessive head motion, 1 for task comprehension failure); all female; mean age = 22.4 years, range 18–28.
- **population_category:** healthy adults
- **population_age_range:** 18–28
- **ecological_validity:** Low — probabilistic slot machine task with stylized social rewards (static face photos + spoken words); no real social interaction; all-female sample limits generalizability.
- **eligibility_flag:** Borderline — the task involves learning stimulus–reward associations where rewards happen to be social (faces) vs. non-social (money). There is no social interaction, social inference, or learning about another agent. The "social" element is purely the reward modality. Flag as: social reward learning but no social agent or social context beyond stimulus type.
- **concerns:** - No free parameters fitted — the "computational model" is essentially a descriptive summary statistic (moving average) rather than a fitted learning model. - Social PE did not survive the omnibus statistical threshold; only shown at a lower threshold (P < 0.005, 5 voxels). - All-female sample — authors note potential gender differences in social reward processing. - No model comparison performed. - Social rewards may not be well-matched in magnitude to monetary rewards. - Small sample size (N = 22).
- **limitations_reported:** Given the limited spatial resolution of fMRI we cannot rule out the possibility that there might be neuronal subpopulations within the vmPFC and ventral striatum specialized in valuing certain types of rewards"; "only females participated in the experiment" limiting generalizability to gender differences in social reward processing; social PE signals did not survive omnibus threshold.
- **limitations_categorized:** limited spatial resolution of fMRI; sample homogeneity (all female); small sample size; weak social prediction error signal; no model comparison; stimulus matching concerns
