## Quality
- **sample_size:** N=25 (11 women; age M = 19.10, SD = 0.36 years; first-year college students; 2 excluded for neurological abnormalities)
- **population_category:** undergraduates
- **population_age_range:** M=19.10 (SD=0.36)
- **ecological_validity:** Low-moderate. Experiment 2 uses abstract fractal stimuli with no social content. Experiment 3 uses trivial self-disclosure statements with real friend/parent targets who would receive emails, increasing ecological validity for the social component. However, all performed in scanner.
- **eligibility_flag:** Does not meet all inclusion criteria. Experiment 2 uses computational modeling with learning over time but the learning context is non-social (alien classification). Experiment 3 is social but has no computational model and no temporal learning. The author explicitly states no economic model was applied to Experiment 3 choices. Flag as: no single study satisfies all four criteria simultaneously.
- **concerns:** - Only one computational model tested (single RW model with one free parameter); no model comparison - No parameter recovery or model recovery reported - Very small sample (N=25) - Experiment 3 explicitly lacks a computational model - Thresholds for Experiment 3 whole-brain results are liberal (uncorrected p < .005, k >= 75) - Dissertation, not peer-reviewed - The conjunction approach links self and reward but does not demonstrate social learning per se - MNI coordinates in Table 1 appear inconsistent between the two threshold sections (the pgACC peak coordinates differ: -6, 10, -8 vs -6, 36, -4)
- **limitations_reported:** No "share with self in the future" condition, so private vs. social comparisons may be contaminated by prospective cognition differences; alternative interpretation that cognitive load rather than social context drives effects; alternative interpretation that attention/salience differences drive effects; sample constrained to first-year college students limiting generalizability; domain content of disclosure statements could be confounded across conditions; individual variation in relationship quality with targets not assessed
- **limitations_categorized:** lack of appropriate control condition; confound with prospective cognition; confound with cognitive load; limited generalizability (age-restricted sample); stimulus confounds; no relationship quality assessment
