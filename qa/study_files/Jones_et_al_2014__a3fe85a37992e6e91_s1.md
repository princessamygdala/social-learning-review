## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Social reinforcement learning — developmental differences in learning from positive social feedback (peer approval) across childhood, adolescence, and adulthood.
- **study_focus_short:** Social reinforcement learning
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from varying probabilities of positive social feedback (peer approval) about peer reinforcement value   - Learning from:     - Source type (social): other (age-matched peers)     - Source content (social): outcome (positive social feedback — receiving a note vs. not)   - Learning about:     - Target type (social): other (specific peers)     - Target content (social): stimulus (reinforcement value / expected probability of positive feedback from each peer)
- **task_description:** Participants viewed faces of three age-matched peers, each associated with a distinct probability (33%, 66%, 100%) of delivering positive social feedback (a "note" indicating interest in the participant). On each trial, a peer's face appeared, the participant responded to a wink detection task (providing RT as a learning index), and then received or did not receive a note from that peer.
- **task_paradigm:** Social conditioning
- **players:** Single agent (participant), multi-target (3 age-/gender-/ethnicity-matched peers)
- **n_players:** multi-target (3+)
- **partner_type:** computer (algorithmic)
- **stimuli:** Photographs of age-matched peers, wink detection cue, note feedback (hand holding note vs. note given to someone else)
- **method:** fMRI / behavioural
- **method_full:** fMRI + behavioural
- **main_result:** - Quadratic age effect on positive learning rate (α+): adolescents showed lower α+ than children and adults (β = 0.22, p < 0.02; β = 0.21, p < 0.04 after removing α+ = 0 individuals) - Linear age effect on α+: increasing age predicted lower α+ (β = −0.26, p < 0.01) - No age effects on negative learning rate α− (p's > 0.09) - Preference ratings: main effect of reinforcement probability on likeability change (F(2,228) = 5.64, p < 0.01) - α+ positively correlated with reversal RT change for Continuous peer (r(117) = 0.23, p < 0.02) - Prediction error (δ) positively correlated with mPFC (peak: −1, 47, 8) and ventral striatum (−7, 8, 2) activity across age — no age differences - Adolescent-specific (quadratic) greater insula activity during positive prediction error processing (peak: −40, 2, 2; 62 voxels) - Adolescent-specific greater putamen (23, −4, 5; 60 voxels) and SMA (15, −34, 53; 49 voxels) activity to receipt of positive social feedback - Insula positive PE correlated with putamen positive feedback activity (r(76) = 0.27, p < 0.02)
