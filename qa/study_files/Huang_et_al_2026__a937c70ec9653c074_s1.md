## Quality
- **sample_size:** N = 92 (from 107 recruited; 58 female, 32 male, 2 unindicated; ages 18–39, M = 24.52, SD = 3.93). 15 excluded for missing SVO data, excessive invalid trials, disbelief in other condition, or random responding.
- **population_category:** healthy adults
- **population_age_range:** 18–39
- **ecological_validity:** Low-moderate. Abstract spaceship/planet cover story; anonymous future participant as "other" recipient reduces ecological validity of prosocial context. Deterministic transitions rather than stochastic (as in Daw et al.) simplify structure. No real social interaction.
- **eligibility_flag:** 
- **concerns:** - Only one computational model tested (no formal model comparison with alternative architectures) - Data and code available only upon request, not publicly shared - "Other" is a completely anonymous future participant — limited generalizability to closer social relationships - Self/Other manipulation is blocked, not interleaved — potential order/fatigue effects (though counterbalanced) - No model recovery (confusion matrix) since only one model tested
- **limitations_reported:** - Did not explore how mental model learning processes differ for self vs. other; only examined model use, not model acquisition; - Dual-system MB/MF distinction may not capture the full spectrum of strategies, especially under high decision noise for others; - Mechanisms underlying SVO–MB link are speculative (inequality aversion vs. cognitive control accounts not disentangled); - Only one recipient type (anonymous stranger) — cannot speak to effects of social distance or closeness; - Task length constraints prevented including additional recipients (e.g., close others, charities)
