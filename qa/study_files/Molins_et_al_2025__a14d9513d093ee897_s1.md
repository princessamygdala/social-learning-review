## Quality
- **sample_size:** N = 73 (stress group N = 35, control group N = 38); age M = 21.2, SD = 3.01; 79.5% women
- **population_category:** healthy adults
- **population_age_range:** M=21.2 (SD=3.01)
- **ecological_validity:** Low-to-moderate. The IGT is a well-established laboratory decision-making task but is abstract (card decks with monetary outcomes). Stress induction used a virtual-reality adaptation of the TSST (TSST-VR), which has ecological limitations compared to real social evaluative threat. The 35-minute delay between stressor and task is designed to capture late HPA-axis effects but the lab setting limits generalizability.
- **eligibility_flag:** The learning context is NOT social. The IGT involves individual reinforcement learning from non-social monetary outcomes. The stress induction (TSST-VR) is social in nature, but the learning task itself does not involve learning in a social context. The paper does not meet the inclusion criterion "learning occurs in a social context." FLAG: non-social learning task; stress manipulation is social but learning/decision-making is non-social.
- **concerns:** - No cortisol measurement — authors acknowledge this as a major limitation; cannot confirm HPA axis activation during IGT - No supplement file accessible despite paper referencing "Supplemental Material section" for VPP model equations - Sex imbalance (79.5% women) limits generalizability - Very large effect sizes for some VPP parameters (η²p = .99 for Memory) are unusual and may warrant scrutiny - The learning task itself is non-social — stress is the social manipulation, but the computational model captures non-social reinforcement learning - Paper refers to participants as N=37 experimental and N=39 control in Table 1 but N=35 and N=38 in the abstract/Methods; likely a discrepancy (possibly 2 excluded from one analysis)
- **limitations_reported:** Absence of hormonal analysis, particularly cortisol measurement, prevents confirming whether cortisol levels peaked during the decision-making task; TSST-VR may have lower impact on HPA axis than original TSST; significant disproportion of women prevents firm sex-based conclusions; no additional measurements during the waiting period between stressor and IGT to verify differential stress progression; data will be made available only on request
- **limitations_categorized:** no hormonal/biological verification; limited ecological validity of stress induction; sex imbalance / limited generalizability; no mid-protocol stress verification; data not openly available
