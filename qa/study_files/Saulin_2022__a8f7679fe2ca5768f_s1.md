## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Empathy learning; sustainability of empathy-related social closeness through reinforcement learning
- **study_focus_short:** Empathy learning
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from observing another's pain/non-pain about social closeness to that person     - Learning from:       - Source type (social): other (confederate partner)       - Source content (social): outcome (pain vs. non-pain stimulation of the other)     - Learning about:       - Target type (social): other (confederate partner)       - Target content (social): state (social closeness / affective bond)
- **task_description:** In an acquisition-extinction paradigm, participants observed painful stimulation of a confederate with high probability (80%, acquisition) then low probability (20%, extinction), and rated their social closeness to the confederate trial by trial. A control condition kept stimulation at 50%.
- **task_paradigm:** Empathy / pain task
- **players:** Single agent (participant), dyadic (confederate partner); separate treatment and control partners
- **n_players:** dyadic (2)
- **partner_type:** confederate
- **stimuli:** Pain stimulation observation (colored flash indicating painful vs. non-painful stimulation), continuous closeness rating scale, emotion rating scale
- **method:** fMRI (Study 1a), behavioural replication (Study 1b), behavioural control for reciprocity (Study 1c)
- **main_result:** - Empathy-related social closeness increased in acquisition and persisted in extinction (no significant decay; T(45) = -0.96, P = .34 for fMRI study)   - Reciprocity-related social closeness decayed during extinction (condition x trial number x block: $\chi^2$ = 120.69, P < .001)   - Bayesian model comparison: recalibration model won with exceedance probability > 99% and expected frequency 97% (fMRI study); replicated in behavioural study   - Recalibration parameter $\omega$ was larger in extinction than acquisition (T(45) = 2.753, P = .009)   - Neural recalibration effect: bilateral STS/TPJ (left peak: x = -66, y = -26, z = 0; right peak: x = 60, y = -16, z = 10) and left IFG/AI (x = -32, y = 16, z = 18)
