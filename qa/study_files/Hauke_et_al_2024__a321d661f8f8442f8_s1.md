## Quality
- **sample_size:** N = 56 (19 HC, 19 CHR-P, 18 FEP); FEP ages mean 33.44 [SD 11.70], HC ages mean 21.37 [SD 2.52], CHR-P ages mean 21.05 [SD 3.52]. FEP were short-term medicated (5.44 +/- 2.79 days).
- **population_category:** mixed
- **population_age_range:** 
- **ecological_validity:** Task uses prerecorded videos from real human-human interactions (ecologically valid social cue); however, the interaction is one-directional (no reciprocal interaction), and the volatile phase schedule is pre-programmed rather than emergent. Authors describe the task as "deception-free and ecologically valid.
- **eligibility_flag:** 
- **concerns:** - Small sample size (N=56 total, 18-19 per group) limits statistical power; key parameter-symptom correlations did not survive multiple comparison correction. - FEP group significantly older than HC and CHR-P (mean 33 vs 21 years), which is a potential confound despite age being included as a covariate. - No non-social control task to determine domain specificity of effects. - Parameter recovery for kappa2, mu2(0), and mu3(0) was adequate in only 55-65% of simulations, raising concerns about reliability of these specific parameter estimates. - Different winning models for different groups complicates direct parameter comparison across groups. - FEP were on short-term antipsychotic medication (though controlled for statistically).
- **limitations_reported:** Small sample size due to selective inclusion criteria regarding medication; cannot assess specificity of results to social domain as no non-social control task was included; cannot speak to diagnostic specificity as no clinical control group was included; similar results reported in autism suggesting mechanism may not be specific to psychosis.
- **limitations_categorized:** Sample size; no non-social control condition; limited diagnostic specificity; limited generalizability; medication confound (short-term)
