## Quality
- **sample_size:** Experiment 1: N = 151 (M age = 39.78, SD = 13.73; 94F, 53M, 4 nonbinary). Experiment 2: N = 295 (M age = 40.02, SD = 12.31; 142F, 149M, 4 nonbinary). Total N = 446.
- **population_category:** healthy adults
- **population_age_range:** 
- **ecological_validity:** Low-moderate; abstract necklace-building task with binary bead sequences is highly simplified relative to real-world cultural transmission of motifs. Online Prolific sample. Teachers limited to single example. Incentivized with performance bonus.
- **eligibility_flag:** Borderline -- the "learning" is single-trial inference from one example rather than learning over time. The learner does not receive repeated feedback or update beliefs across trials. The teacher makes 18 one-shot decisions (one per village) but there is no trial-by-trial updating of a learning parameter. This is closer to one-shot pedagogical inference/communication than learning over time. FLAG: borderline learning-vs-decision-making; may be one-shot inference rather than temporal learning.
- **concerns:** The core task involves single-example inference rather than learning over time (no trial-by-trial updating with feedback). The pedagogical sampling model is a communication/inference model, not a learning model per se. No parameter recovery or model recovery analyses reported. Mean fitted alpha values not reported in main text. Supplement referenced but not accessible for extraction.
- **limitations_reported:** Task limits teachers to providing a single example, which obscures ways learners may refine abstract concepts over longer interactions; motifs in the task were simple sequences without real-world meaning; models do not capture how meanings of motifs constrain deployment; left-to-right bias may be culturally specific; pedagogical sampling model does not fully capture what makes human teaching effective.
- **limitations_categorized:** task simplicity; limited ecological validity; single-example constraint; no temporal learning dynamics; model incompleteness; potential cultural specificity
