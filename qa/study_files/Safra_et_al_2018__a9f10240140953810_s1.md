## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Social influence on reward learning; audience effect on reinforcement learning in depression; social observation learning
- **study_focus_short:** Social influence on reward learning
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from one's own reward outcomes and a demonstrator's choices/outcomes about stimulus-reward associations, modulated by social context (presence of demonstrator information)   - Learning from:     - Source type (non-social): self       - Source content (non-social): outcome (reward/punishment feedback on own choices)     - Source type (social): other (virtual demonstrator)       - Source content (social): action/policy (demonstrator's choices) and outcome (demonstrator's reward outcomes)   - Learning about:     - Target type (non-social): world       - Target content (non-social): stimulus (stimulus-reward probability associations)
- **task_description:** Participants performed a probabilistic reinforcement learning task in which they chose between two abstract symbols associated with reciprocal reward probabilities (20/80% or 30/70%) across three conditions: Private (no social info), Social-Choice (demonstrator's choice visible), and Social-Choice+Outcome (demonstrator's choice and outcome visible). The demonstrator was a virtual agent controlled by a Q-learning algorithm.
- **task_paradigm:** Probabilistic reward learning
- **players:** Single agent (participant), dyadic (virtual demonstrator partner)
- **n_players:** dyadic (2)
- **partner_type:** computer (algorithmic)
- **stimuli:** Abstract symbols (agathodaimon font characters), binary reward feedback (+1/−1 points), avatars (neutral faces)
- **method:** online / behavioural
- **method_full:** behavioural / online
- **main_result:** - Depression scores specifically reduced correct choice rates in Social-Choice condition vs Private (t(489) = −2.64, p = .009) - Effect replicated in both discovery and replication samples (exploration: r = −.29; replication: r = −.37) - Demonstrator performance positively influenced participant performance in social conditions (Social-Choice: t(495) = 2.70, p = .007; Social-Choice+Outcome: t(495) = 2.25, p = .025) - Perceived trustworthiness of demonstrator predicted social learning (Social-Choice: t(98) = 3.17, p = .002) - Model-free classifier: 76% accuracy identifying severe depression from Social-Choice vs Private performance difference - Model-based classifier: 79% accuracy, 84% sensitivity from learning rate difference
- **effect_size:** - Social-Choice x Depression interaction: β = −0.05 (SE = 0.02) - Exploration sample correlation depression-Social-Choice performance: r = −.29 - Replication sample correlation: r = −.37 - Model-based classifier accuracy: 79 ± 1%, sensitivity: 84 ± 1%, specificity: 53 ± 3% - Model-free classifier accuracy: 76 ± 1%, sensitivity: 78 ± 2%, specificity: 63 ± 3% - Depression effect on social learning rate (SEM): z = −2.41
