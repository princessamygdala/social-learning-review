## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Observational learning; social learning strategies; neural correlates of social vs self-guided reinforcement learning; memory encoding
- **study_focus_short:** Observational learning · social learning strategies
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from own and observed others' choice outcomes about cue-reward associations, with neural correlates of social learning strategies and their impact on memory encoding.     - Learning from:       - Source type (non-social): self (own experienced choices and outcomes)       - Source type (social): other (fictitious partner's observed choices and outcomes)       - Source content (non-social): outcomes (reward/no reward feedback)       - Source content (social): outcomes (observed partner's reward/no reward)     - Learning about:       - Target type (non-social): world (cue-reward contingencies)       - Target content (non-social): stimulus (which fractal cue is associated with higher reward probability)
- **task_description:** Participants performed a probabilistic decision-making task in an fMRI scanner, choosing between two fractal cues (experienced trials) or observing a fictitious partner's choices (observed trials), with congruent (80:20) and incongruent (70:30 reversed) contingency pairs. - **main_result:** - Social ignoring group learned both congruent and incongruent pairs; Social learning group failed incongruent pair (group x pair interaction: F(1,39) = 50.9, p < 0.0001)   - Relative LR difference correlated with incongruent trial performance (r = 0.87, p < 0.0001)   - Observed > experienced trials activated bilateral social cognition network (MTG, angular gyrus/TPJ; p < 0.001 uncorrected)   - Social learning group showed heightened activation in social cognition ROIs vs Social ignoring group in Run 2 (R angular: F(1,39)=5.75, p<0.05; L angular: F(1,39)=5.64, p<0.05; R MTG: F(1,39)=4.3, p<0.05)   - Subsequent memory effects (remembered > forgotten) in bilateral extrastriate (R: F(1,39)=23.52, p<0.00005; L: F(1,39)=18.49, p<0.0005) and fusiform cortices, stronger during observed vs experienced trials   - Social learning group: confidence ratings parametrically correlated with encoding activation in visual areas during observed trials   - Social learning group exhibited higher subjective memory strength than Social ignoring group (F(1,39)=4.983, p<0.05), particularly during observed trials (F(1,39)=4.054, p<0.05)
