## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Prosocial learning; interaction between empathy and financial incentives on the prosocial decision process
- **study_focus_short:** Prosocial learning
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from empathy induction (with or without financial incentive) about prosocial decision efficiency     - Learning from:       - Source type (social): other (confederate partner)       - Source content (non-social): outcome (pain observation + monetary bonus) — **joint** in empathy-bonus condition     - Learning about:       - Target type (social): other (confederate partner)       - Target content (social): action/policy (prosocial vs. egoistic allocation)
- **task_description:** After empathy induction (observing a confederate's pain), participants performed a binary dictator game. In one condition (empathy-alone), decisions were empathy-based only. In the other (empathy-bonus), participants were additionally offered a financial bonus for making prosocial decisions in the majority of trials. Within-subjects design.
- **task_paradigm:** Dictator game
- **players:** Single agent (participant), dyadic (confederate partner; 2 confederates, one per condition)
- **n_players:** dyadic (2)
- **partner_type:** confederate
- **stimuli:** Pain stimulation observation (colored flash), binary point distributions (dictator game with advantageous inequality, 60:40 vs. 90:10 self:other ratios), financial bonus (5 Euro)
- **method:** fMRI
- **main_result:** - More prosocial decisions in empathy-bonus (M = 85.65%) vs. empathy-alone (M = 73.92%; $\chi^2$ = 14.35, P < .01, R$^2_m$ = .08)   - DDM: v-parameter larger in empathy-bonus (M = 2.03) vs. empathy-alone (M = 1.25); p(v-bonus > v-alone) = .99   - z-parameter and a-parameter: no significant differences between conditions   - Empathy ratings x condition interaction on prosocial decisions: $\chi^2$ = 10.23, P = .001; lower empathy → greater incentive benefit   - Neural: v-parameter in empathy-bonus correlated with left AI (x = -27, y = 38, z = 5, T = 6.32, 5% FWE voxel-based)   - Condition x v-parameter x empathy ratings interaction in AI ($\chi^2$ = 5.97, P = .01)
- **effect_size:** - Prosocial decision frequency difference: $\beta$ = -0.57, SE = 0.15, R$^2_m$ = .08   - Empathy rating x condition interaction on decisions: $\beta$ = -0.43, SE = 0.13, R$^2_m$ = .05   - Difference in prosocial decisions ~ empathy ratings: $\beta$ = -0.36, SE = 0.17, R$^2$ = .13   - AI: v-parameter x empathy x condition 3-way interaction R$^2_m$ = .20
