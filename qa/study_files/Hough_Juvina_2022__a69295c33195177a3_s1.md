## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Cooperation/coordination learning — learning to coordinate effort choices in a group coordination game (minimum effort game) via signaling strategies, counterfactual thinking, and belief formation about others' choices.
- **study_focus_short:** Cooperation/coordination learning
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from group members' effort choices and payoff outcomes to coordinate on a joint equilibrium in a minimum effort game   - Learning from:     - Source type (social): group (other 3 players in the 4-person group)       - joint: choices are interdependent; payoff depends on group minimum     - Source content (social): action/policy (effort choices of all group members) and outcome (payoffs determined by own choice and group minimum)       - joint: outcomes are jointly determined   - Learning about:     - Target type (social): group (predicting other players' future choices)     - Target content (social): action/policy (other players' predicted effort choices) and state (beliefs about others' player types / strategies)  [HIGH — directly described in model architecture and data analysis]
- **task_description:** In a 20-round minimum effort game, four players simultaneously choose an effort level (1-7) without communication; each player's payoff is determined by their own choice and the group minimum choice, creating a coordination dilemma with Pareto-ranked Nash equilibria. After each round, players are shown all choices and counterfactual payoffs.

- **players:** Multi-agent (4-person groups), symmetric; 18 groups (N=72 participants)
- **n_players:** network (5+)
- **partner_type:** human (live)
- **stimuli:** Numeric effort choices (1-7), payoff matrix, counterfactual payoff information
- **method:** behavioural

## Algorithmic level
- **winning_model:** PSS (Prediction, Strategy, and Simulation) model in ACT-R: instance-based learning for player choice predictions via blending mechanism; 4 strategies (min, ave, max, signal) with utility learning: U_i(n) = U_i(n-1) + alpha[R_i(n) - U_i(n-1)]; blending: V = min sum P_i * (1-sim(V,V_i))^2; 2 player types (RD, PD); counterfactual simulation of unchosen strategies with cfw = .75
