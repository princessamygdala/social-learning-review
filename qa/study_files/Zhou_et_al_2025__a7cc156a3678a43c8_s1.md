## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Ingroup bias learning; how framing interactions as gains vs. losses affects learning-related changes in intergroup impressions via reinforcement learning.
- **study_focus_short:** Ingroup bias learning
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from monetary outcomes produced by ingroup/outgroup members about closeness to and impressions of those group members   - Learning from:     - Source type (social): other (ingroup or outgroup member)       - Source content (non-social): outcome (monetary gain/loss)   - Learning about:     - Target type (social): other (ingroup member) / other (outgroup member)       - Target content (social): state (mental state; impression/closeness)
- **task_description:** Participants interacted with ingroup (Swiss) or outgroup (Middle Eastern) confederates who could give (Gain frame) or take (Loss frame) money, with identical net payoffs across frames. Over 96 trials, participants rated closeness and expectancy, and impressions were measured pre- and post-learning.
- **task_paradigm:** Impression formation task
- **players:** Single agent (participant), multi-target (6 ingroup Swiss confederates, 6 outgroup Middle Eastern confederates); between-subjects framing (Gain vs. Loss).
- **n_players:** multi-target (3+)
- **partner_type:** confederate
- **stimuli:** Room labels (A/B) indicating ingroup/outgroup, monetary outcomes (0 or 5 MU in Gain frame; 5 or 10 MU taken in Loss frame), manikin closeness scale.
- **method:** behavioural
- **method_full:** behavioural
- **main_result:** - Frame x Group x Time interaction on impression ratings (beta = -0.024, 95% CI [-0.05, -0.002], chi-squared = 4.78, p = 0.029) - In Loss frame: significant Group x Time interaction (chi-squared = 11.5, p < 0.001, beta = 0.09); ingroup bias eliminated after learning - In Gain frame: no significant Group x Time interaction (chi-squared = 0.42, p = 0.52); ingroup bias persisted - W_neg_in predicted intergroup impression change in Loss frame (beta = -0.13, t(52) = -2.0, p = 0.05; Spearman rho = -0.37, p = 0.013) but not Gain frame (beta = 0.06, t(56) = 0.80, p = 0.43) - Frame moderated W_neg_in effect on ingroup impression change (beta = -0.23, t(107) = -2.32, p = 0.022)
- **effect_size:** - Frame x Group x Time: beta = -0.024 - Loss frame Group x Time: beta = 0.09 - W_neg_in -> impression change (Loss frame): rho = -0.37 - Frame moderation on W_neg_in -> ingroup impression: beta = -0.23
