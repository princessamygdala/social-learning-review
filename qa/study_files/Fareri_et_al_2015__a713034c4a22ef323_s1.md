## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Trust learning / collaboration learning -- how social value of reciprocation, modulated by relationship closeness, drives collaborative (trust) decisions over repeated interactions.
- **study_focus_short:** Trust learning / collaboration learning -- how social value of reciprocation
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from partner reciprocation/defection outcomes about the value of collaboration with different relationship partners   - Learning from:     - Source type (social): other (partner -- friend, confederate, or computer)     - Source content (social): outcome (reciprocation or defection)   - Learning about:     - Target type (social): other (partner)     - Target content (social): state (social value / trustworthiness of collaboration with partner)
- **task_description:** Participants played an iterated trust game with three partners (close friend, confederate stranger, computer) while undergoing fMRI. On each trial, participants chose to keep $1 or invest (tripled to $3 for partner), and the partner either reciprocated (even split) or defected; all partners reciprocated at a fixed 50% rate.

- **n_players:** network (5+)
- **partner_type:** confederate
- **stimuli:** Facial photographs of partners, monetary outcomes ($0 / $1.50)
- **method:** fMRI
- **method_full:** fMRI
