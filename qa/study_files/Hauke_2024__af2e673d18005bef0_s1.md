## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Learning from advice / social learning under volatility in emerging psychosis (paranoid delusions). How first-episode psychosis (FEP) and clinical high-risk (CHR-P) individuals learn about a social partner's changing intentions.
- **study_focus_short:** Learning from advice / social learning under volatility in emerging psychosis
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from an adviser's changing recommendations about the adviser's helpfulness/fidelity over time   - Learning from:     - Source type (social): other (adviser)     - Source content (social): action/policy (advice accuracy -- helpful vs. misleading recommendations)   - Learning about:     - Target type (social): other (adviser)     - Target content (social): state (mental state; adviser's intentions/fidelity -- tendency to help vs. deceive)
- **task_description:** Participants predicted the outcome of a binary lottery on each trial. They received two information sources: a non-social cue displaying the true winning probabilities and a social cue (prerecorded video of a human adviser giving a recommendation). The adviser's helpfulness changed over time across stable and volatile phases.
- **task_paradigm:** Advice-taking task
- **players:** Single agent (participant), dyadic (adviser -- prerecorded videos from prior human-human interaction)
- **n_players:** dyadic (2)
- **partner_type:** human (live)
- **stimuli:** Binary lottery, pie chart (non-social cue showing outcome probability), prerecorded videos of human adviser giving recommendations
- **method:** behavioural

## Quality
- **sample_size:** N = 56 (19 HC, 19 CHR-P, 18 FEP); FEP mean age 33.44 [SD 11.70], HC mean age 21.37 [SD 2.52], CHR-P mean age 21.05 [SD 3.52]. FEP were short-term medicated (5.44 +/- 2.79 days).
- **population_category:** mixed
- **population_age_range:** M=33.44
- **ecological_validity:** Task uses prerecorded videos of real human advisers from prior human-human interactions, enhancing ecological validity compared to abstract cues. However, interaction is not truly interactive (prerecorded), and the binary lottery structure is simplified relative to real-world social advice-taking. Authors note that real-world such interactions may unfold over much longer time-scales.
- **eligibility_flag:** 
- **concerns:** - FEP group significantly older than HC and CHR-P (mean 33 vs. 21 years), which is a major confound despite age being included as a covariate - Small sample sizes per group (n = 18-19) - Key parameter effects (m3, kappa2) did not survive Bonferroni correction for multiple parameters - Symptom-parameter correlations did not survive Bonferroni correction - kappa2 parameter recovery was poor (only 55% of simulations met criterion) - No non-social control task to assess domain specificity - FEP were on short-term antipsychotic medication
- **limitations_reported:** Small sample size due to selective inclusion criteria regarding medication; cannot assess specificity to social domain since no non-social control task was included; cannot speak to diagnostic specificity since no clinical control group was included; similar results have been reported in autism populations
- **limitations_categorized:** sample size; no non-social control condition; limited diagnostic specificity; limited generalizability
