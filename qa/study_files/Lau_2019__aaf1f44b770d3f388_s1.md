- **task_description:** Participants assigned to an arbitrary team (Eagles) categorized political (Democrat/Republican) and arbitrary (Eagles/Rattlers) in-group and out-group members in a repetition suppression fMRI paradigm. - **task_paradigm:** Minimal group paradigm
- **players:** Single agent (participant), multi-target (targets from 4 groups: Eagles, Rattlers, Democrats, Republicans)
- **n_players:** multi-target (3+)
- **partner_type:** unclear

- **method:** fMRI
- **main_result:** - Frontoparietal control network (bilateral SPL, bilateral DLPFC, bilateral MTG) exhibited repetition suppression for in-group targets (out-group/in-group > identical in-group, different in-group)   - Negative correlation between group valuing tendency and repetition suppression in bilateral DLPFC (r(20) = -0.47, p = 0.027 for right DLPFC; r(20) = -0.38, p = 0.076 for left DLPFC)   - 34.3% overlap between repetition suppression map and independently identified FPCN (Spreng et al., 2010)   - Repetition enhancement in rTPJ and insula for in-group targets
