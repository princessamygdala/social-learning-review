## Quality
- **sample_size:** N = 46 (36 fMRI, 10 behavioural only); 3 excluded from fMRI for poor performance/noncompliance → 33 fMRI analysed; ages 18–28; 22 females total (19 females in fMRI sample).
- **population_category:** healthy adults
- **population_age_range:** 18–28
- **ecological_validity:** Moderate — used a cover story to make social feedback believable (peers choosing to pass notes), but the interaction is artificial (no real peers, no bidirectional interaction, predetermined reinforcement schedules). The wink detection task is not naturalistic social behaviour.
- **eligibility_flag:** 
- **concerns:** - Only one model tested (no model comparison) - Group-level parameter fitting may obscure individual differences - No model recovery, parameter recovery, or simulation reported - Coordinates in Talairach space (not MNI) - Deception paradigm — participants believed feedback was from real peers - RT as sole behavioural index of learning (no choice data) - OFC result only survived small volume correction, not whole-brain
- **limitations_reported:** Did not assess whether no-reinforcement trials were experienced as mildly rejecting; did not examine developmental differences (adolescents vs adults); the OFC was not sensitive to expected values at whole-brain corrected thresholds; RT measure indexes approach behaviour rather than explicit choice/strategy.
- **limitations_categorized:** Task simplicity; limited ecological validity; no developmental comparison; liberal statistical threshold for some results; single behavioural measure; no model comparison.  ---  ### 6.
