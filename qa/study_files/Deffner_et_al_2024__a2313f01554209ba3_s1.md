## Quality
- **sample_size:** N = 160 group foragers (40 groups of 4; 20 groups individual incentives, 20 groups group incentives) + 40 individual foragers as control; 63 male, 97 female in group condition; M_age = 28.5, SD_age = 6.4 years
- **population_category:** healthy adults
- **population_age_range:** 
- **ecological_validity:** High ecological validity for a lab study — immersive 3D virtual environment with naturalistic spatial/perceptual constraints (limited first-person field of view, realistic movement speeds), continuous free interaction among group members, endogenously generated social cues. Authors explicitly discuss ecological validity as a design priority, noting that cues arise naturally from participant behavior rather than being externally imposed.
- **eligibility_flag:** Potential concern — the paper models latent strategy switching (producer vs. scrounger states) over time, but the "learning" is about dynamic calibration of social information use strategies rather than classic trial-by-trial value updating. The model infers latent states and how situational factors influence transitions, rather than tracking evolving value estimates. This is borderline learning-vs-decision-making: participants do adjust strategies over rounds (temporal dynamics show calibration), but the computational model itself is a state-switching model, not a learning model with prediction errors or belief updates. FLAG: borderline learning-vs-decision-making.
- **concerns:** - No traditional model comparison (only one computational model architecture tested; no competing model families compared) - Exact number of free parameters not enumerated in main text or supplement - No parameter recovery analysis reported for the full hierarchical model (only baseline state recovery on synthetic data mentioned in preregistration) - Model is bespoke and tailored to this specific paradigm — generalizability to other tasks unclear - The "learning over time" is demonstrated via monotonic time trends in decision weights, but the model itself does not include explicit learning dynamics (no prediction errors, no value updating) - Evidence ratios used instead of conventional effect sizes; some posterior contrasts have wide credible intervals
- **limitations_reported:** Authors note that the paradigm creates zero-sum scrounging scenarios and suggest future work on cooperative scenarios where social information facilitates novel group-level abilities; group size was fixed at 4 and should be varied; patches of different qualities were not included; the model is tailored to the present paradigm (especially state-dependent variables); individual search processes were not varied; real-world ecological validity of experimental cues determines ecological validity of the experiment itself
- **limitations_categorized:** task simplicity (fixed group size, uniform patch quality); limited generalizability (bespoke model for specific paradigm); limited ecological validity (zero-sum competition only); no parameter recovery (for full model); task simplicity (no resource quality variation)
