## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Stay/leave decision-making; social partner choice; reinforcement learning in social vs. nonsocial contexts
- **study_focus_short:** Stay/leave decision-making
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from monetary outcomes provided by social partners (Dictators) or slot machines about the expected value of staying with vs. leaving each option.   - Learning from:     - Source type (social): other (4 ostensible Dictators in social condition)     - Source type (non-social): world (4 slot machines in nonsocial condition)     - Source content (non-social): outcomes (monetary rewards)   - Learning about:     - Target type (social): other (generosity/expected value of each Dictator)     - Target type (non-social): world (expected reward of each slot machine)     - Target content (non-social): outcomes (expected monetary value)
- **task_description:** Participants chose among 4 options (avatars representing Dictators in the social condition, or slot machines in the nonsocial condition) on each of 100 trials per condition, receiving monetary rewards (0--50 cents) after each choice; rewards fluctuated across trials via a Gaussian random walk, and participants could stay with the previously chosen option or switch.
- **task_paradigm:** Multi-armed bandit
- **players:** Single agent (participant), multi-target (4 ostensible Dictators in social; 4 slot machines in nonsocial)
- **n_players:** multi-target (3+)
- **partner_type:** unclear
- **stimuli:** Avatars (social) or slot machine icons (nonsocial); monetary outcomes (0--50 Euro cents); coin pile visual feedback
- **method:** fMRI
- **method_full:** fMRI
- **main_result:** - EV of chosen option predicted stay/leave decisions (F(1,4) = 672.00, p < .001, beta = -1.16) - Condition x EV interaction: EV was a stronger predictor in nonsocial than social condition (F(1,4) = 28.11, p < .001, beta = -0.51) - RL model with dual learning rates predicted choices above chance in both conditions (social NLL M = 95.01; nonsocial NLL M = 100.07 vs. chance = 138.67) - Participants learned faster from negative (alpha_NEG M = 0.95) than positive (alpha_POS M = 0.66) prediction errors (t(1,24) = 10.32, p < .001) - vmPFC encoded relative EV of chosen options (P = .013 FWE cluster-level after SVC) - Social > nonsocial stay decisions: left caudate nucleus (k = 94, Z = 5.06, P = .003 FWE); left calcarine sulcus (k = 283, Z = 4.37, P < .001 FWE); left superior medial frontal gyrus/vmPFC (k = 59, Z = 3.75, P = .021 FWE); septo-hypothalamic region (k = 7, Z = 3.58, P = .019 FWE SVC) - No significant clusters for social > nonsocial leave (AI), nor for social > nonsocial overall (TPJ, mPFC)

## Quality
- **sample_size:** N = 26 (1 excluded for technical problems; final N = 25); mean age 23.04, range 19--30; 50% female

- **ecological_validity:** Low-moderate. Social interactions were minimal (passive receipt of Dictator Game offers from ostensible partners). Participants believed they interacted with real people, but social exchange was restricted to monetary outcomes with no direct social feedback. Authors explicitly acknowledge the task was "rather minimally social and not particularly naturalistic.
- **eligibility_flag:** 
- **concerns:** Only one model tested (RW with dual LRs) -- no alternative models compared; no parameter recovery or model recovery; small sample (N = 25); social condition not truly interactive (participant is passive recipient); small volume corrections used for key hypotheses; no significant behavioral difference in stay rates between social and nonsocial conditions (the hypothesized "stay bias" was not directly supported behaviorally, only via EV x condition interaction and neural differences).
