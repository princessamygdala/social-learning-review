## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Social categorization learning -- how environmental feedback structure and individual differences (racism, social justice orientation) interact to shape whether participants form group-based vs. person-based (individuated) representations of novel social groups.
- **study_focus_short:** Social categorization learning -- how environmental feedback structure and
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from feedback about novel others' cooperative behavior to form category representations (group-based vs. person-based) of social group members.   - Learning from:     - Source type (social): other (alien group members)     - Source content (non-social): outcome (cooperation: give or take points)   - Learning about:     - Target type (social): other (individual aliens within two groups)     - Target content (social): action/policy (cooperation likelihood)
- **task_description:** Participants played a game where they encountered individual members of two visually distinct alien groups (characterized by skin colour) one at a time, choosing whether to interact with each. If they interacted, the alien gave or took points; cooperation rates varied both between and within groups. In the partial feedback condition, feedback was contingent on approach; in the full feedback condition, participants learned the alien's action regardless.
- **task_paradigm:** Categorization task
- **players:** Single agent (participant), multi-target (12 aliens across 2 groups of 6 each).
- **n_players:** multi-target (3+)
- **partner_type:** computer (algorithmic)
- **stimuli:** Cartoon alien faces with green or blue skin, binary monetary feedback (+1 or -1 points).
- **method:** online / behavioural
- **method_full:** behavioural / online
- **main_result:** - Main Results:   - Participants in partial feedback condition formed fewer person-based clusters for the bad group, especially extreme members (3-way interaction group x person type x condition: b = 0.028, SE = 0.012, t = 2.27, p = .024)   - Those higher in symbolic racism formed fewer person-based clusters only in partial feedback condition (racism x condition interaction: b = 0.14, SE = 0.064, t = 2.15, p = .032)   - Those lower in internal motivation to control prejudice formed fewer person-based clusters in partial feedback (IMP x condition: b = -0.077, SE = 0.035, t = -2.20, p = .029)   - Social justice orientation predicted person-based clusters specifically for extreme bad-group members in partial feedback (4-way interaction: b = -0.033, SE = 0.014, t = -2.28, p = .023)   - Need for closure showed overall main effect on person-based clusters (b = -0.16, SE = 0.09, t = -1.98, p = .048) regardless of condition   - Number of person-based clusters predicted explicit cooperation ratings (3-way interaction group x alien type x clusters: X2 = 10.37, p = .006)
