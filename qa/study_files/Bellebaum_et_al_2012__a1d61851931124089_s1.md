## Quality
- **sample_size:** N=30 (15 active learners: 9 women, mean age 26.0, SD=4.1; 15 observational learners: 10 women, mean age 26.2, SD=4.9)
- **population_category:** healthy adults
- **population_age_range:** M=26.0
- **ecological_validity:** Low; abstract symbol learning with monetary outcomes in scanner; observational learning from pre-recorded (yoked) performances rather than live social interaction; minimal social contact between observer and observed.
- **eligibility_flag:** 
- **concerns:** Very small sample (N=15 per group); between-subjects design limits power for direct comparisons; PE-fitting procedure is unconventional (boundary-value algorithm from test trials rather than likelihood-based fitting); no formal model comparison; only one model tested; PE parametric modulator conflates stimulus visual properties with PE; statistical threshold liberal (p<.001 uncorrected, 8 voxel extent); supplement tables (S1-S5) referenced but not accessible — full coordinate tables and additional activations are in those tables; no model validation or recovery analyses.
- **limitations_reported:** Cannot exclude that subjective reward value differed between active and observational learners despite matched instructions; between-subjects design prevents direct within-subject comparison; the coding of unexpected vs. expected outcomes relies on 50% probability stimuli which are not truly "unexpected" but rather unpredictable; visual appearance differences between reward/non-reward stimuli may contribute to PE-related activations in visual cortex; no clear results obtained for structures specifically recruited during observational learning; differential hippocampal role requires further exploration.
- **limitations_categorized:** sample size; between-subjects design; limited ecological validity; no model comparison; no parameter recovery; potential confound (reward value asymmetry); potential confound (stimulus visual properties); unconventional model fitting
