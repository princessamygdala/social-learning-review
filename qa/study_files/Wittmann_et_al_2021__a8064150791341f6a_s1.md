## Computational level
- **study_focus:** Self-other mergence — how estimates of one's own ability become confused with estimates of others' abilities as a function of social context (cooperation vs. competition), and the causal role of dmPFC in separating self and other representations.
- **study_focus_short:** Self-other mergence
- **learning_mode_description:** - Learning mode: Learning from own and others' performance feedback to form ability estimates that become context-dependently merged across self and other.   - Learning from:     - Source type (non-social): self       - Source content (non-social): outcome (performance feedback for self)     - Source type (social): other (co-player)       - Source content (social): outcome (performance feedback for other)   - Learning about:     - Target type (non-social): self       - Target content (non-social): state (self-ability estimate)     - Target type (social): other (co-player)       - Target content (social): state (other-ability estimate)
- **task_description:** Participants tracked performance feedback for themselves and two other players across minigame trials, rating expected performance for self and a relevant other on each trial. Before each minigame, participants made engage/avoid decisions in either a cooperative or competitive context with one of the other players, with payoffs dependent on combined or differential performance relative to a threshold.
- **task_paradigm:** Social comparison task
- **players:** Single agent (participant), multi-target (2 other players; cooperative and competitive context)
- **n_players:** multi-target (3+)
- **partner_type:** unclear
- **stimuli:** Abstract minigames (reaction-time-based perceptual tasks), parametric performance feedback on 15-point scale, initials of players, engage/avoid decisions with cooperative/competitive framing
- **method:** fMRI
- **method_full:** fMRI + cTBS (continuous theta burst stimulation)
- **main_result:** - Main Results:   - SOM_int(S/O) present in baseline no-cTBS data: O-ability influenced by S-performance more positively in cooperation than competition (t(55) = 3.233)   - SOM_int(O/S) present in baseline: S-ability influenced by O-performance more positively in cooperation than competition (t(55) = 2.162)   - cTBS over dmPFC reduced neural SOM_int(S/O) signal in dmPFC (z = 3.94, peak 6/46/30)   - cTBS over dmPFC reduced S-performance signal during competition in dmPFC (z = -4.27, peak -4/46/24; survived whole-brain correction)   - cTBS over dmPFC increased behavioral SOM_int(S/O): group × stimulation interaction (F(1,54) = 6.681, η² not reported)   - Within dmPFC group, SOM_int(S/O) increased with cTBS (t(27) = 2.578)   - No significant correlation between neural and behavioral SOM_int(S/O) change (r = -0.205)
- **effect_size:** Effect sizes reported as t-statistics and z-values; F(1,54) = 6.681 for critical behavioral interaction. No Cohen's d, r², or η² explicitly reported.

## Quality
- **sample_size:** N = 56 (28 dmPFC group, 28 vertex group; 65 initially recruited, 9 excluded); age range 18–39 years; 26 female. Each participant completed 2 fMRI sessions (cTBS and no-cTBS), yielding 112 sessions total.
- **population_category:** healthy adults
- **population_age_range:** 18–39
- **ecological_validity:** Low ecological validity. Task uses abstract minigames with predetermined performance feedback; other players are described as pre-recorded rather than live interaction. Social context (cooperation/competition) is experimenter-assigned rather than participant-chosen. However, the engage/avoid decision provides some agency.
- **eligibility_flag:** Borderline: RL model is ancillary (generates regressors only); SOM is described as decision-related, not learning-related. The paper does involve trial-by-trial performance learning via RL, but the primary phenomenon under investigation (self-other mergence) is a context-dependent representational bias, not learning per se.
- **concerns:** - Only one RL model tested; no model comparison - Model fitted at group level (one α, one β per group of 28), not individually — reduces sensitivity - Mean fitted parameter values not reported - No parameter recovery or model recovery performed - SOM is explicitly described as decision-related, not learning-related (line 230) - Effect sizes for the critical behavioral interaction (F(1,54) = 6.681) not reported as standardized effect sizes (no η², d) - cTBS intensity differed between dmPFC and vertex groups (mean 35.5% vs 42.2%)
- **limitations_reported:** Authors note that the cTBS effect was particularly pronounced during competition, possibly because S-performance was especially strongly represented under competition in the dmPFC during no-cTBS; authors acknowledge that the neural-behavioral correlation within the dmPFC group was not significant (r = -0.205); interpretation of cTBS effects is limited by the indirect nature of stimulation effects on neural activity.
- **limitations_categorized:** limited ecological validity; no model comparison; group-level fitting; no parameter recovery; no standardized effect sizes; stimulation intensity mismatch between groups; non-significant brain-behavior correlation
